#### ⏱️ MINUTE 4: Independent Zero-Trust Safety Validation & Controller Decision
- **Action:** Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:3000/validation` and then `http://127.0.0.1:3000/review`.
- **Narration for Judges:**
  > *"In railway operations, AI can NEVER be an unchecked black box. RailSync AI enforces a strict architectural boundary:*
  > 1. **Rule Validator Independence:** *The solver's output is sent to a separate, deterministic Python validation engine that checks 100% of IR safety constraints independently—including minimum 10-minute headway buffers, 25kV traction de-energization footprints, crew mandatory rest periods (14-hour rolling duty checks), and machine travel times.*
  > 2. *The validator returned `STATUS: PASS`.*
  > 3. **Human Authority Boundary:** *RailSync AI is strictly a Decision Support System (`SOFTWARE_PROPOSAL_ONLY`). The software CANNOT grant line possession. Only the human Section Controller can review the validation report, enter their digital authorization, and approve the plan.*
  >
  > *Here, the Chief Controller at Prayagraj Division has reviewed the cryptographic snapshot, verified the shadow block, and granted approval."*

- **Visual Evidence on Screen:**
  - Validation Report with green checkmarks for Headway, Resource Rest, and Footprint Safety.
  - Approved Controller Decision Card with audit timestamp, user role `CONTROLLER`, and immutable ledger state.
